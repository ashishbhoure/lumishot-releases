Screenshots for Windows that look good enough to post.

Press a key, drag over anything on your screen, and it lands on a clean background with rounded corners and a shadow, ready to paste into Slack, GitHub or a doc.

Download for Windows

Free. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

Capture

  • Any region, on any screen. The screen freezes so menus and tooltips stay put while you drag.
  • Scrolling capture. A whole web page or chat in one tall image, scrolling down or up, with sticky headers kept once.
  • Copy text from the screen. Drag over an error message, a video or a scanned PDF and the text is on your clipboard. Works offline, and reads QR codes too.
  • Colour picker and ruler. See the exact colour under the pointer and copy it, or measure any distance in real pixels.
  • A whole window. Hover and click; it comes out cut from the desktop, with rounded corners and a soft shadow.
  • Timer. Three, five or ten seconds to open a menu first.

Record

  • Region or full screen, with computer sound and your microphone.
  • Auto-zoom. The video glides in towards each click and follows your pointer, so a demo looks edited without editing.
  • Captions, made on your PC. Lumishot writes down what you said, in English and many other languages. Edit them, show them in the video, or save an .srt.
  • Hide private info. One click finds emails, phone numbers and keys in the video and blurs them while they show. Or blur and spotlight any area yourself.
  • Draw as you talk. Circle and underline things on the screen while recording; the lines fade by themselves.
  • Shortcuts on screen. Keys you press, like Ctrl+C, show in the video. Plain typing never does.
  • Step-by-step guides. Turn a recording into a written how-to: every click becomes a numbered step with a picture, saved as a PDF or web page.
  • Ready for Reels and Shorts. Put the video on a background, in any shape, even vertical 9:16, with click ripples and a webcam bubble.
  • Cut, speed up, trim. Take out the slow parts, play it at up to 4×, and share it as MP4 or GIF.

Share

  • A thumbnail after every capture. Copy, save, edit, or drag it straight into a chat.
  • Mark it up. Arrows (straight or curved), boxes, numbered steps, a magnifier, highlights that fit the text, pen, text styles, blur and crop. Move or restyle anything afterwards.
  • Your background. 35 gradients and colours, your own gradient or picture, or your wallpaper blurred, in a Mac, browser or Windows window frame. Save looks as presets.
  • Pin it. Keep a screenshot floating over your other windows while you work.
  • Find any screenshot by its text. Search the last fifty captures for anything they showed, like an error message. Read offline, kept on your PC.
  • Before and after. Put two screenshots side by side, labelled, to show what changed.

Everything is one shortcut away

AltShiftA Every capture mode in one bar
AltShiftS Capture a region
AltShiftW Capture a window
AltShiftF Capture the full screen
AltShiftR Start or stop recording
AltShiftL Scrolling capture
AltShiftT Copy text from the screen

Change any of them in Settings.

Get Lumishot

Download for Windows

If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. Lumishot is new and not code-signed yet, so Windows does not know it yet.

Your captures stay on your PC. No account, nothing uploaded, and text recognition and captions run offline. Lumishot only goes online to check for updates, which install themselves when you quit, and to download the speech model the first time you make captions.
